## About

I am an **NLP/AI researcher and research software developer** working at the intersection of Natural Language Processing, responsible and explainable AI, digital humanities, and computational social science.

I work primarily in **Python** for research software, NLP pipelines, data analysis, reproducible workflows, and model experimentation. I use Python-focused libraries and tooling (PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, spaCy, Jupyter) as my main coding environment.

My work focuses on developing practical, reproducible, and accessible tools for analysing language, narratives, speech, and knowledge-rich datasets. I am particularly interested in **low-resource languages, explainable AI, and digital humanities**.

I currently work as a **Research Fellow at Lancaster University**, contributing to research involving NLP, large language models, responsible AI, explainability, and digital humanities.
